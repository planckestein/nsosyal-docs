# NSosyal Innovation Competition — Competitive Feature Landscape

## Purpose

This document is **competitive product reconnaissance**, not yet a list of the most requested user features.

The goal is to examine what established social platforms such as **X, Bluesky, Threads, Reddit, Discord, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Mastodon, and others** already ship, then identify mechanics that could be adapted into **NSosyal**.

The guiding question is:

> **What proven social-product patterns could NSosyal adopt, improve, or combine into something distinctive enough for the NSosyal İnovasyon Yarışması?**

---

## What NSosyal Already Has

Before proposing anything, it is important to avoid duplicating functionality NSosyal already ships.

Based on its current product/release history, NSosyal already includes:

- Text posts
- Images and video
- Polls
- Discover / Trends
- Direct messages
- Stories
- Reply controls
- Profile search
- Pinned posts
- Verification applications
- A media-focused feed / “Media Mode”
- Detailed CV-style profiles
- “Küre” long-form/article functionality

Source:

- [NSosyal on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=tr&id=com.ntech.app)

This means ideas such as **“add DMs,” “add Stories,” or “add long posts”** are weak competition directions because they largely duplicate existing functionality.

# Competitive Feature Mine

| Concept to adapt for NSosyal | Proven implementation elsewhere | Possible NSosyal version |
|---|---|---|
| **User-selectable algorithms / feed marketplace** | Bluesky lets users subscribe to custom feeds generated by different algorithms. Algorithmic choice is one of Bluesky's defining product ideas. | **Akışlar**: Sports, local news, chronological, friends-only, science, “quiet accounts,” etc. Users could pin several as tabs. Advanced users could potentially publish feed recipes. |
| **Natural-language algorithm control** | Threads' **Dear Algo** lets users tell the recommendation system what they want more or less of. Threads has also expanded feed-control work with **Your Algo**. | **Akışıma Söyle**: “For three days, show me more F1 and less politics.” This maps naturally to the **Social AI** category. |
| **Starter Packs** | Bluesky Starter Packs bundle recommended people and custom feeds into one shareable onboarding package. | Packs such as **ODTÜ students**, **Turkish game developers**, **defense industry**, **Fenerbahçe**, **beginner programmers**, etc. One click gives a new user a useful network instead of an empty feed. |
| **Real Communities rather than hashtags** | X has moderated Communities. Threads also has Communities with flairs, community identities, and community recognition mechanics. | **NSosyal Toplulukları** with moderators, rules, member flair, community feeds, contributor status, and potentially community-specific recommendation algorithms. |
| **Community onboarding questionnaire** | Discord Community Onboarding asks newcomers questions and automatically gives them relevant roles/channels. | Join a “Technology” community → answer **AI / cybersecurity / mobile / hardware** → NSosyal automatically configures relevant subtopics and feeds. |
| **Live Event Mode** | Threads has live chats around major events. X has Spaces. Reddit supports structured scheduled AMAs. | A football match, election, TEKNOFEST event, launch, or breaking-news event gets a temporary **Event Hub** containing live posts, chat, polls, Q&A, score/info cards, and possibly audio. |
| **Structured AMA / Q&A** | Reddit supports scheduled AMAs, guest collaborators, RSVPs/reminders, and Answered/Unanswered filtering. | Verified scientists, politicians, athletes, engineers, etc. host an **NSoru** session. Questions are ranked, answered status is explicit, and the discussion becomes a clean permanent archive. |
| **Community Notes / crowdsourced context** | X Community Notes lets contributors add context to potentially misleading posts and uses cross-perspective agreement mechanisms. | **Bağlam Notları**, potentially with reputation/expertise weighting and transparent reasoning. Strong trust/safety potential if differentiated from a direct clone. |
| **Distributed verification / expertise verification** | Bluesky allows independent **Trusted Verifiers** and domain-based identity. | Universities verify students/faculty, professional bodies verify engineers/doctors, clubs verify players, companies verify employees. Verification becomes **domain-specific credibility**, not just a generic blue tick. |
| **Composable moderation** | Bluesky supports moderation labels from services chosen by users. Mastodon provides granular keyword/phrase filters. | User-selected moderation packs: **hide football spoilers**, **blur politics**, **filter gambling**, **strict anti-spam**, etc., instead of one centralized all-or-nothing moderation model. |
| **Pre-post smart checks** | Reddit's Post Check warns users if a submission may violate community rules and can suggest a more appropriate destination. | Composer assistance: “This belongs in #Teknoloji rather than #Bilim,” “You may be exposing a phone number,” “This community requires a source.” Concrete, useful AI rather than generic generation. |
| **AI search grounded in actual discussions** | Reddit's AI-powered Answers/search summarizes relevant conversations while linking back to original discussions. | Ask: **“What do people on NSosyal think about the new YKS system?”** → clustered summary, competing viewpoints, cited posts, and links to originals. |
| **Broadcast Channels** | Instagram Broadcast Channels provide one-to-many creator communication with text, media, voice, polls, replies/prompts, and collaborators. | Creators, news organizations, clubs, and teams get a **high-signal channel** separate from the noisy public timeline. Followers explicitly subscribe to updates/notifications. |
| **Newsletters / recurring publications** | LinkedIn lets users create newsletters that people subscribe to separately and receive notifications for. | Build on **Küre** with recurring **Küre Dizileri / Bültenleri**, scheduled editions, subscribers, archives, and analytics. |
| **Co-authored posts** | Instagram Collabs lets multiple accounts jointly author one post distributed to the collaborators' audiences. | Joint announcements, interviews, project launches, or publications credited to several NSosyal accounts instead of one account posting and tagging the rest. |
| **Remix / response media** | TikTok Stitch lets users take part of another creator's video and append their own contribution with attribution. | Video replies/remixes with explicit source lineage. Could also apply to long-form posts: highlight a paragraph of a Küre article and respond to it. |
| **Low-pressure ephemeral posting** | Threads has disappearing **Ghost Posts**. Instagram has experimented with temporary/ephemeral sharing formats. | Temporary **normal-looking conversations**, not another Story clone. Engagement can be private and the post automatically archives after a chosen time. |
| **Fine-grained profile privacy** | Reddit lets users choose which communities' posts/comments appear publicly on their profile. | Because NSosyal already has CV-like profiles, users could keep professional/academic posts visible while hiding casual community participation from the public profile without deleting it. |
| **Better measures of community health** | Reddit has emphasized current Visitors/Contributions rather than relying only on historical subscriber totals. | Communities could show metrics such as **8.2k active this week / 2.1k contributors / 68% questions answered** rather than meaningless accumulated member counts. |
| **Federated/interoperable identity and content** | Threads supports fediverse interoperability. Bluesky is built around an open protocol and portable ecosystem. | Follow compatible external accounts, portable identity, public API/feed ecosystem, or controlled interoperability. Technically ambitious but potentially very distinctive. |

# Strongest Competition Project Families

Without yet considering **user demand**, five categories stand out.

## 1. Feed Control / Algorithm Transparency

Combine:

- Bluesky-style custom feeds
- Threads-style natural-language feed control
- Transparent recommendation settings

Possible direction:

> Let users **build, select, and conversationally modify their NSosyal algorithm** instead of passively accepting one recommendation feed.

Examples:

- “Show me more AI for the next week.”
- “Hide football until Monday.”
- “Make this feed chronological.”
- “Show only people I follow plus posts liked by at least three of them.”
- “Create a feed for Turkish indie game developers.”

Why this is interesting:

- Proven product concept
- Strong Social AI fit
- Easy to demonstrate visually
- Addresses algorithm-control concerns directly
- Can become substantially more powerful than either Bluesky custom feeds or Threads' simple natural-language controls

---

## 2. Event Mode

Social platforms generate huge bursts of activity around:

- Football matches
- Elections
- Exams
- Earthquakes
- Breaking news
- Concerts
- TEKNOFEST
- Product launches
- Television events
- Major cultural moments

Instead of forcing all of that through the normal timeline, NSosyal could generate a **temporary structured social environment**.

Possible Event Hub:

- Live posts
- Live chat
- Polls
- Q&A
- Score/information cards
- Official sources
- Community Notes/context
- Audio rooms
- Media gallery
- Timeline of important developments
- “What happened while I was away?” AI summary

Why this is interesting:

- Extremely demonstrable
- Fits how Turkish social media is heavily used around live events
- Combines several proven platform mechanics into one coherent feature
- Could be differentiated rather than copied

---

## 3. Trust Layer

Combine:

- X Community Notes
- Bluesky Trusted Verifiers
- Domain/expertise verification
- Reputation systems

Instead of merely asking:

> “Is this person verified?”

NSosyal could answer:

> **“Why should I trust this person on this subject?”**

Examples:

- A university verifies a professor as faculty
- A hospital verifies a doctor
- TMMOB verifies an engineer
- A football club verifies its players/staff
- A company verifies employees
- A subject expert accumulates high-quality community-note reputation

Possible display:

**Dr. Ayşe Yılmaz**
- ✓ Identity verified
- ✓ Hacettepe University Faculty
- ✓ Medical expertise
- 93% helpful-context rating

Why this is interesting:

- Trust is a core social-network problem
- Strong differentiation potential
- Combines identity and contextual expertise rather than a generic status badge
- Could integrate with anti-misinformation features

---

## 4. AI That Operates on the Social Graph

Avoid generic “write a post with AI” features.

More useful AI could operate **on the network and its information**.

Examples:

### Social Search

> “What do NSosyal users think about the new YKS system?”

Output:

- Major viewpoints
- Supporting posts
- Opposing posts
- Common arguments
- Sentiment distribution
- Links to original posts

### Smart Routing

Before posting:

> “This question would probably get better answers in Yazılım > React.”

### Duplicate Detection

> “17 people asked essentially the same question today. Join the existing discussion?”

### Perspective Mapping

> “There are three major positions in this discussion.”

### Trend Explanation

Instead of just:

> `#Something is trending`

show:

> **Why it is trending**, when the spike began, major contributing posts, and competing interpretations.

### Conversational Feed Control

> “Less politics, more science and programming until Friday.”

Why this is interesting:

- Makes AI useful instead of decorative
- Strong Social AI category alignment
- Grounded in actual NSosyal data
- Produces impressive demos
- Can reduce spam, repetition, and information overload

---

## 5. Community Infrastructure

Combine:

- Bluesky Starter Packs
- X/Threads Communities
- Discord onboarding
- Reddit flair
- Reddit activity metrics

The problem:

> **A user signs up. Now what?**

New social networks often struggle because the first experience is an empty or irrelevant feed.

Possible system:

### Starter Pack

“Turkish Software Developers”

Includes:

- 30 recommended accounts
- Programming feed
- Relevant communities
- Recommended publications
- Events

### Community Onboarding

Questions:

> What interests you?

- Frontend
- Backend
- AI
- Cybersecurity
- Mobile
- Game development

Then NSosyal configures:

- Community memberships
- Feed tabs
- Suggested accounts
- Notification preferences

### Community Reputation

Show:

- Active this week
- Contributors
- Questions answered
- Median response time
- Helpful contributors
- Community health

Why this is interesting:

- Directly attacks the cold-start problem
- Encourages retention
- Improves discovery
- Combines multiple proven mechanics into a coherent onboarding ecosystem

# Ideas That Are Probably Too Weak Alone

These may still work as components, but probably should **not** be the entire competition project:

- DMs
- Stories
- Long-form posts
- Basic polls
- Generic chatbot
- Generic “AI writes your post” button
- Basic hashtag improvements
- Dark mode
- Simple profile redesign
- Another TikTok-style feed
- Basic badges
- Basic notifications
- Basic recommendation algorithm
- Generic verification badge

Most are either already present in NSosyal or too commonplace to demonstrate meaningful innovation.

---

# Current Strategic Takeaway

Do **not** choose the project yet.

This research only covers the **supply side**:

> **What have successful social platforms already learned to build?**

The next research layer should cover the **demand side**:

> **What do users repeatedly ask these platforms to add, complain is missing, or beg them to change?**

The strongest project opportunity should lie at the intersection of:

> **Proven feature pattern + unresolved user pain + something NSosyal does not already have + feasible prototype + obvious competition-demo value**

That intersection is where the actual competition project idea should emerge.
